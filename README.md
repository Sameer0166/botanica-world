## 📖 Overview

Most agricultural and culinary resources show either a grocery ingredient on a plate *or* a botanical field guide—rarely side-by-side. **Botanica World** bridges that gap for both **students** and **adults** by pairing every single species with **two synchronized visuals**:

1. **Left Visual (`🌿 Living Plant / Tree / Vine`):** How the crop grows in nature (orchard trees, shrubs, climbing vines, paddy grasses, or aquatic roots).
2. **Right Visual (`🍎 Edible Harvest / Crop`):** What is harvested, traded, cooked, and eaten across global kitchens (fruits, vegetables, whole grains, pulses, nuts, seeds, herbs, and spices).

Engineered as a self-contained, single-file web application (`index.html`), it runs offline-ready on mobile phones, tablets, and classroom desktops with zero build steps, zero logins, and zero external dependencies.

## ✨ Complete Feature Breakdown

### 🖼️ 1. Dual-Visual Discovery Engine (440 Paired Visuals)
* **Multi-Stage Wikimedia & Wikipedia Media Pipeline:** Automatically resolves distinct, non-duplicate real photography for both the living plant and its harvested edible form, with `localStorage` caching and concurrency throttling.
* **Data Saver & Offline Vector Mode (`📶`):** One-click toggle between **Real Dual Photos** and zero-bandwidth **Botanical Vector Diagrams** for offline study or slow mobile data.
* **Side-by-Side Comparison Tray (`⚖️`):** Pin any two species (e.g., *Turmeric vs. Galangal* or *Quinoa vs. Amaranth*) to compare morphology, origins, nutrients, and culinary uses in a split modal.
* **Instant Multi-Field Search & Revision List (`★`):** Search simultaneously across English names, Hindi names, Spanish names, Latin scientific names, vitamins, origins, and dishes—and star any specimen to build a personal **Revision List**.

### 🔊 2. 10-Language Pronunciation & Full-Lesson Audio
* **Web Speech API Integration:** Native voice pronunciation in **English (US, UK, India), Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, and Japanese**.
* **Dual-Speed Controls & Full Narrator:** Every card includes **🐢 Slow (0.56×)** for syllable-by-syllable clarity and **⚡ Fast (1.02×)** for natural speech, plus a **🔊 Read Full Lesson Aloud** button inside every detail modal.
* **Interactive Spelling & Cue Cards:** Clicking any card opens letter-by-letter spelling tiles, phonetic syllable breakdowns, botanical memory clues, and mastery toggles (`✓ Mastered`).

### 🧪 3. Daily Habits, Gamified Labs & Assessment Arena
* **🔥 Daily 5-Plant Lesson & Streak Tracker:** Automatically curates 5 fresh specimens each day and tracks consecutive learning days.
* **🥗 Interactive Plant Nutrition & Meal-Builder Lab:** Complete guided dietary missions (e.g., *Vitamin C & Immunity Rainbow Bowl*, *Complete Plant Protein & Iron Thali*, *Brain & Heart Omega-3 Mix*) with **live nutrient progress meters** and per-ingredient diagnostic feedback.
* **🃏 Spaced-Repetition Flashcard Deck:** Flip-card practice deck prioritizing starred revision items with one-tap mastery tracking (`✓ Got It!` vs. `🔁 Need Practice`).
* **♾️ Endless 3-Hearts Survival Arena:** High-stakes identification streak challenge with persistent all-time high-score tracking.
* **🎯 6-Mode Quiz Arena & Canvas Certificate Generator:**
  1. *Plant & Harvest Detective*
  2. *Listen & Spell*
  3. *Culinary & World Uses*
  4. *Botanical Nutrition*
  5. *World Origin Geography*
  6. *Grand Botanist Mix*
  * Scoring **≥ 70%** unlocks a custom-rendered HTML5 Canvas **Botanical Mastery Certificate** downloadable as a high-resolution `.png` or printable landscape `.pdf`.

### 🌍 4. World Biome Map, Accessibility & Portable Progress
* **Interactive SVG World Biome Map (`🗺️`):** Filter all 220 species across 6 global agricultural cradles (*Americas & New World*, *Mediterranean & Fertile Crescent*, *East & Central Asia*, *Africa & Savanna Tropics*, *Indian Subcontinent*, and *Southeast Asia & Oceania*).
* **Inclusive Study Controls:** Includes **Student Mode**, **Memory Hide** (blurs names until hover for active recall), **Dark Mode (`🌙`)**, **High-Legibility Dyslexia Spacing (`Aa`)**, **Print-Optimized 2-Column Study Sheets (`🖨️`)**, and **Save / Load Progress Codes (`💾 / 📂`)**.
